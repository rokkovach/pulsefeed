How limits work

Last hour: each accepted illustration run counts, including runs whose prompt step fails. This rolling window is separate from daily image attempts. Reset allowances clears spent starts and visitor hourly attempts; recent active or unresolved starts remain counted until they end or age out of this rolling window.

Today: used means confirmed terminal image attempts, including retries. Reserved covers active or unresolved work. Charged includes used, reserved and conservative unknown charges. New illustrations reserve up to two attempts. These are resource controls, not billing totals.
